Does it fit the jar?
Yes — the dipper was chosen for the jar’s wide mouth and is short enough to rest inside with the lid off.
How do I clean it?
Rinse in warm water and dry upright. No dishwasher. A drop of food-safe mineral oil once a year keeps the wood happy.
